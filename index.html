About

If you wonder what happens when a dreamer learns to code, I might be the answer.

After studying Digital Game Design at Bahcesehir University for a year, I realized that the part of the field that truly fit me was software development. That led me to make what was, at the time, a big decision for me: changing both my university and the country I lived in.

While looking for the right program and university, I came across Riga Technical University. I started studying Computer Systems there and have successfully completed two years. I am now in my final year, and along the way I have learned a lot about both software and hardware.

Throughout that process, I kept wanting to turn what I learned into real projects, and I did. I created this site to catalogue and present that work in a more organized and thoughtful way.
